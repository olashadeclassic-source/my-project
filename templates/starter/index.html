About the Book

When Elara Whitmore inherits her grandmother's crumbling Victorian estate, she expects peeling wallpaper and dusty memories. What she doesn't expect is the garden — a lush, impossible paradise that blooms only under the light of the midnight moon.

As Elara tends the mysterious garden, she discovers each flower holds a memory from her family's past — and a hidden door behind the old oak tree leads to a world she never knew existed. But the garden is fading, and with it, the last threads of magic in her bloodline.

With the help of a reclusive botanist and a cryptic journal written in her grandmother's hand, Elara must race against time to save the garden before the final petal falls — uncovering a century-old secret that will change everything she thought she knew about love, sacrifice, and the stories we leave behind.
